## 1. YouTube trailer

**Title**

Rift Armada – Closed Beta Trailer | Space RTS for Android

**Description**

The gates went silent. Then something came through.

Rift Armada is a real-time space strategy game built for phones and tablets. Build your base, mine ice and ore, and lead whole fleets into battle – from fast frigates to capital ships.

Rift Armada is currently in closed testing on Google Play, and we are looking for testers. Joining is free and takes a minute:

1. Join the tester group: https://groups.google.com/g/rts_tester
2. Opt in to the test: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.riftarmada.game
3. Install the game from Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.riftarmada.game

Use the same Google account for all three steps. The store page only opens after you have joined the group and opted in.

What is in the beta:
• Five factions that play differently: The Alliance, the Martian Republic, the Golden Legion, The Nexus and The Brood
• A story campaign plus short sorties with star ratings
• A daily challenge with a streak
• Skirmish battles against the AI and a wave survival mode
• Jump gates, artillery, carriers, hero ships and capital ships
• No ads, no pay-to-win, and it plays offline

Tell us what works and what doesn't – the game has a built-in feedback form, or write to rtsgame2026@gmail.com. Every report from the beta shapes the release.

All footage was captured from the game. This is a beta, so things may still change.
